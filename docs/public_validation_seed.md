# Public Documentary Validation Seed Set

These are **candidate source domains**, not labeled ground-truth cases. They are intentionally kept
outside the developmental benchmark. A separate case author and adjudicators should construct the
case materials without access to DIAGNOSE mechanism labels.

## Candidate A — Boeing 737 MAX 9 mid-exit door plug blowout
Primary public source:
https://www.ntsb.gov/news/press-releases/pages/nr20250624.aspx

Why useful:
The NTSB attributes the event to inadequate training, guidance, and oversight and also identifies
ineffective FAA oversight and weaknesses in Boeing's safety-management system. The record therefore
supports multiple organizational levels without requiring a single simplistic "culture" diagnosis.

Validation use:
Have an independent author construct evidence waves from public NTSB material, then ask adjudicators
to specify mechanisms in free text before mapping them to any DIAGNOSE category.

## Candidate B — DCA midair collision / FAA ATO safety-management and culture findings
Primary public sources:
https://www.ntsb.gov/news/Testimony/Pages/Homendy-20260212.aspx

Why useful:
The public record includes workarounds, rejected safety proposals, reporting concerns, formal safety
systems, management practices, and organizational structure. It is well suited to testing competing
culture, governance, workflow, and information-flow explanations.

Validation use:
Use only public documentary evidence and reveal it in waves. Keep the NTSB's causal conclusions hidden
until after participant diagnosis where possible.

## Candidate C — Coast Guard Integrated Health Information System failure
Primary public source:
https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-18-59

Why useful:
GAO reports financial, technical, schedule, personnel, governance, and documentation failures in a
multi-year system-acquisition effort. This provides a strong interacting-mechanism case.

Validation use:
Independent case authors should separate contemporaneous observations from GAO's retrospective
interpretation so the task does not simply reproduce the report's conclusions.

## Candidate D — Crosscutting federal performance-management challenges
Primary public source:
https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-108601/index.html

Why useful:
GAO describes long-standing coordination and integration problems across crosscutting federal
activities. This is useful for testing structural/coordination explanations where no single focal
organization controls all dependencies.

Validation use:
Construct a case around one documented cross-agency performance challenge rather than using the
general report as a single case.

## Candidate E — Norfolk Southern organizational safety practices
Primary public source:
https://www.ntsb.gov/news/press-releases/pages/nr20230323.aspx

Why useful:
The NTSB opened a special investigation because of multiple significant accidents and explicitly
examined organizational safety practices and culture.

Validation use:
Prefer final investigative materials, where available, over the opening press release. Do not prelabel
the case as a "culture" problem merely because that language appears in the investigation title.

## Independence rule
These source domains are only seeds. To preserve independence:
1. the person constructing the case should not receive the DIAGNOSE ontology;
2. free-text mechanism labels should be collected before ontology mapping;
3. ground truth and adjudication should be stored separately;
4. no confirmatory case should be used to tune prompts, weights, or thresholds.
